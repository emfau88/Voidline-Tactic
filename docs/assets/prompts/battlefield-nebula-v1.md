# Battlefield Nebula v1 – Generationsspezifikation

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: portrait game environment background for a mobile-first 2D tactics battlefield

Primary request: an original deep-space void background with a restrained abyssal-nautical astronomical identity

Scene/backdrop: very dark near-black space; a soft desaturated violet gas shelf entering from the upper-left edge; a deeper blue-teal cloud mass near the lower-right edge; sparse faint dust structures; open low-contrast center for tactical ships and overlays

Style/medium: polished hand-painted 2D game environment texture, cinematic but restrained, no photorealistic telescope look

Composition/framing: 2:3 portrait, edge-weighted atmosphere, generous calm negative space through the central combat corridor, seamless-feeling crop edges

Lighting/mood: cold, quiet, ominous; no bright central light source

Color palette: near-black navy, muted deep violet, petrol teal and tiny cold-white star accents

Constraints: opaque full-frame background; no text, UI, border, logo or watermark; no ships, weapons, asteroids, planets or recognizable constellations; retain enough dark space for cyan/red silhouettes and translucent weapon arcs

Avoid: saturated neon, dense Milky Way, bright lens flare, giant planet, central focal object, excessive stars, colorful fantasy clouds, baked tactical grid
